1. Game Overview

Rummikub is a tile-based game that combines elements of rummy and mahjong, where players strategically form and manipulate sets and runs of tiles on the board.

Objective

The objective of Rummikub is to be the first player to use all the tiles from your player board by forming them into valid series on the game board.

Players

Rummikub can be played with 2 to 4 players. The game is most enjoyable with 3 or 4 players, but the 2-player version offers a more strategic experience.

2. Game Setup

Starting the Game

When the game starts, each player is assigned 14 tiles from the bank. These tiles are placed on your player board and are only visible to you.

The Bank

The bank contains all the remaining tiles that have not been distributed to players. When players are required to draw a tile, they take it from this bank.

Turn Order

The current playing player is marked by a white glowing circle on their icon. Play proceeds in a clockwise direction.

3. Tiles & Series

Tile Definition

A tile is a card with a value ranging from 1-13 and one of the following colors: red, orange, blue, or black. There are 2 tiles of each color-value combination in the game.

4. Playing the Game

Initial Meld

To start playing tiles, you must first make an initial meld with a total value of 30 or more. The value is calculated as the sum of all tiles you place on the board in your first move.

Important: A joker's value is always counted as 0 for the purpose of reaching the 30-point threshold.

Adding a Series

To add a new series to the board:

  1. Select at least 3 tiles from your player board that form a valid series.
  2. Click the add (+) button to place them on the game board.

Adding to Existing Series

You can add tiles to series already on the board:

  1. Select the tile you want to add from your player board.
  2. Right-click on the existing series on the board.
  3. Select "Add Tile" from the context menu.

5. Game Mechanics

Turn Actions

During your turn, you can:

  • Create new series with your tiles (minimum 3 tiles per series)
  • Add tiles to existing series on the board
  • Rearrange tiles on the board (as long as all series remain valid)
  • Split existing series into smaller valid series
  • Use jokers to substitute for any tile

Turn End Conditions

To end your turn, every series on the board must be valid. If you cannot or choose not to play any tiles, you must draw one tile from the bank.

Reset Button

If you make a mistake during your turn or cannot create valid series with the moves you've made, you can click the reset button to return the board to its state at the beginning of your turn.

6. Advanced Rules

Joker Usage

Jokers are powerful tiles that substitute for any tile. However, they come with special rules:

  • A joker's point value is always 0 for the initial meld.
  • You can replace a joker on the board with the exact tile it represents from your player board.
  • After replacing a joker, you must immediately use it in another valid series on the board.

Board Management

During your turn, you can rearrange tiles on the board in any way, as long as all series remain valid at the end of your turn. This allows for complex moves like:

  • Breaking a 4-tile series to use one tile elsewhere
  • Combining tiles from multiple series to form new ones
  • Extending series with tiles from your player board

Admin Privileges

An Admin player can remove tiles freely by selecting them and clicking the remove button. This feature is useful for game management and correction of errors.

7. Game End & Scoring

Finishing the Game

When you successfully place all your tiles on the board and end your turn with 0 tiles, you have finished the game. You will need to wait until all other players finish or the game ends.

Game End Conditions

The game ends when:

  • All players except one have finished (used all their tiles)
  • The bank is empty and a player needs to draw a tile

Scoring & Ranking

After each game, players receive a score based on their performance. These scores determine your ranking in the leaderboard.

Experience Points

In addition to scores, you also earn experience points (XP) from games, which determine your level. Your level is a reflection of your overall gameplay experience and cannot be negative.

Surrender Penalty

If you surrender during a game, you automatically receive a point penalty. To surrender, click the menu button and select "Surrender".

8. Online Features

Public Queue

Join the public queue to be matched with other players looking for a game.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard displays the top players ranked by their score. You can check your own position and stats by visiting the leaderboard page.

Private Games

There are two ways to play privately with friends:

  1. Private Lobby: Create a private game lobby with a unique game code. Share this code with friends to allow them to join your game.
  2. Friend Invites: For a faster method, go to the friends page, right-click on the friend you want to play with, and select "Invite to Game" for a quick 1-on-1 match.
